The 5-Day Confidence Reset

One small practice a day. Takes 10 minutes. By Ella, Confidence & Mindset Coach.

Day 1: Notice

Write down every self-doubting thought you catch today. Don't fight them, just notice.

Day 2: Reframe

Turn each thought into a kinder, truthful one. "I'll fail" becomes "I'm learning and I can try."

Day 3: Keep a promise

Make one tiny promise to yourself and keep it. Self-trust is built on evidence.

Day 4: Speak up

Share one opinion or say one honest "no" today.

Day 5: Celebrate

List 5 wins from this week. Read them aloud. You're already showing up.
